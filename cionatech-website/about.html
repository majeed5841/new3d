How Ciona Tech Started

Founded in Houston, Texas, Ciona Tech LLC was born from a simple belief: every business deserves access to enterprise-level digital marketing strategies.

What began as a small team of passionate marketers has grown into a full-service digital agency serving clients across Houston, Sugar Land, Katy, The Woodlands, and beyond. Our journey started when our founders recognized a gap in the market for transparent, results-focused marketing partnerships.

Today, we pride ourselves on being more than just a service provider—we're strategic partners invested in our clients' success. Our Houston roots keep us grounded, while our innovative approach pushes boundaries.
